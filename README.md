# Implementing-Machine-Learning-for-Optimizing-Debit-Product-Marketing

## Annotation
This bachelor’s thesis presents a comprehensive investigation into the development of a data-driven marketing solution for promoting a specialized debit product—the Junior Card—at T-bank. By leveraging advanced machine learning (ML) techniques, this work aims to predict which users are most likely to acquire the Junior Card and, crucially, to determine the optimal communication channel (email, SMS, chat, banner, or push notification) for reaching each user. The approach combines traditional supervised learning algorithms—such as Logistic Regression, Support Vector Machines (SVM), Random Forests, and Gradient Boosting—with uplift modeling to measure the incremental benefit of specific marketing treatments. Comparative experiments indicate that CatBoost, supplemented by Bayesian channel scoring, offers both improved predictive accuracy and stronger uplift metrics compared to other models in the study. In addition to the model’s design, the thesis discusses its seamless integration into T-bank’s existing campaign management system, highlighting the potential for significant cost savings and improved marketing effectiveness.
## Key words:
Banking, targeted marketing, machine learning, uplift modeling, CatBoost, comparative analysis, financial services, channel optimization
